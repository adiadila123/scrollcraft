The Long Way North

Five days on foot along a coast that does not want to be hurried.

Nobody walks this coast for the view on the first day. The first day is rain, and a path that keeps losing its nerve at the edge of the fields. The view comes later, and only to people who kept going.

Day one

The harbour

We left before the cafés opened. The harbour wall was the last straight line we would see for a while.

Day two

The salt marsh

The path turned into boardwalk and then into an opinion. We followed the posts and trusted the birds.

Day three

The cliffs

Up, for most of the morning. At the top the wind took every sentence and gave back only the vowels.

Day four

The forest

Quiet, after the cliffs. Moss on everything, and a stream that ran beside us like it was also late.

Day five

The lighthouse

We saw it an hour before we reached it. It did not get closer for most of that hour, and then it did.

“The view comes later, and only to people who kept going.”

At the lighthouse there was a bench, and on the bench there was nothing to do. We sat on it anyway, for longer than any of the days had taken to describe.
