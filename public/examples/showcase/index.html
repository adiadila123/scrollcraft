Atelier Sora · Stoneware

Thrown by hand.

One vase, made from a single piece of clay. Scroll, and watch it being made.

Scroll

01

A lump ofwet clay.

Every Sora starts the same way: one weighed piece of stoneware, wedged by hand until there is no air left in it.

02

Pulled upon the wheel.

Opened, then raised in three pulls. The rings a potter’s fingers leave stay until the wall is smoothed.

03

Trimmed aday later.

When the clay is leather-hard, the foot is cut. The vase sits lower, lighter and steady.

04

Fireduntil it glows.

Twice through the kiln. The second firing is hot enough to turn clay into stone.

05

Dippedin glaze.

Held by the foot and dipped once. Where the glaze stops, the bare clay shows: no two lines are alike.
