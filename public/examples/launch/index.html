Questions

Where should it go?

In the middle of the room, or the middle of the table. It has no front, so it does not need a wall behind it.

How do I pair my phone?

Hold the button until the ring turns blue, then choose Orbit One in your phone’s settings.

Can two play together?

Yes. Hold the button on the second one while the first is playing, and they share the song.
