    </Row>
    <Row cls="rv sticky-k" num="Context" title="Give it the facts it cannot guess">

    </Row>
    <Row cls="rv sticky-k" num="Constraints" title="Set the limits">
      <p>Exact library versions, which properties may be animated, the reduced motion rule, the browsers that matter. Constraints are the cheapest way to avoid rework.</p>
    </Row>
    <Row cls="rv sticky-k" num="Output" title="Name the format">
      <p>“The three files, nothing else” stops a long explanation you do not need. Ask for a short plan first when the task is large.</p>
    </Row>
    <Row cls="rv sticky-k" num="Check" title="Ask for evidence">
      <p>End with “list what you tested and what you could not test”. It makes gaps visible instead of hidden.</p>
    </Row>

      }
    />
  </div>
